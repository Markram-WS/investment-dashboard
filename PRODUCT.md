# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Both individual retail investors and professional traders/fund managers using the system for portfolio management across manual and bot-traded positions. Retail investors want clarity and confidence in their positions at a glance; professionals need granular controls for risk, rebalancing, and order management. The primary task is monitoring portfolio health and taking action (transfer cash, rebalance, close orders, adjust risk parameters).

## Product Purpose

A unified dashboard for manual + bot trade portfolio management that provides real-time visibility into positions, P&L, risk metrics, and liquidity. Success means users can assess portfolio health instantly, execute informed decisions (transfers, rebalancing, order management), and trust the data without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Bold, energetic, playful. The canary yellow leads — approachable pastel accents (teal, coral, blue) on a clean white/gray canvas keep the data front and center while injecting warmth and confidence. The voice is direct and specific, not aphoristic. Functional clarity beats decorative flourish.

## Anti-references

Keep the current Miro-inspired direction: clean surfaces, bold yellow anchor, pastel accents, black-primary pill buttons. Avoid fintech-navy-and-gold clichés, dark-mode-only terminal aesthetics, and SaaS-cream minimalism.

## Design Principles

- **Data first, chrome second.** Every visual decision serves readability of the numbers. Color guides attention to what changed or needs action, not decoration.
- **One bold anchor.** The canary yellow is the single loud note — everything else stays in the muted-to-accent range so yellow carries real signal weight.
- **Confidence through consistency.** Recurring patterns (cards, badges, gauges, pills) reduce cognitive load. Users navigating between portfolios and analytics should feel the same muscle memory.
- **Playful but precise.** Pastel accents and subtle animations (pulse, shimmer, gauge draw) make the tool feel alive without undermining the seriousness of financial data.
- **Action-oriented.** Every screen has a clear next action. Buttons say what they do (verb + object). Empty states guide creation, not dead-end.
